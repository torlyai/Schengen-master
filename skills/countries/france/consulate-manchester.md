# TLScontact Manchester (France)

**Address:** Tournament Court, Edgewater Road, Manchester M41 7HA
**Nearest station:** Manchester Piccadilly (15 min by tram + walk) or Trafford Bar (Metrolink, 10 min walk)
**Opening hours:** Mon-Fri 09:00-15:00 (last appointments 14:30)
**Collection hours:** Mon-Fri 14:00-16:00
**Coverage:** Northern England, Midlands, Wales (alternative)
**Last reviewed:** 2026-05-24

## What's here

Manchester is the second-busiest UK TLS centre for France, but considerably less competitive than London. Often a better choice if you're in the Midlands, Yorkshire, or the North West.

## Booking calendar

- Opens 14 days ahead.
- Fewer last-minute releases than London — Visa Master Premium has less effect here, but still useful.
- Group bookings (4+) more easily accommodated than in London due to lower base demand.

## On the day — Manchester specifics

- Trafford Park area; mostly car-friendly. Free parking on site (~30 spaces, first-come).
- The centre is at a business park; not many cafés nearby. Bring water/snacks.
- Waiting area: 30-40 seats; usually has space.
- Bag check on entry; standard.
- Toilets inside.
- WiFi: TLScontact-Guest.

## When to choose Manchester

✅ You live in the North West, Yorkshire, North East, Midlands, North Wales
✅ You're flexible on date but want shorter lead time than London
✅ You prefer driving + free parking
✅ You're applying as a family group (4+) and London is fully booked

❌ You're in Scotland (Edinburgh is closer)
❌ You're in central London (the travel time isn't worth it)
❌ You need afternoon slots — Manchester closes earlier than London

## Local logistics tips

- Free parking on site is the main advantage.
- Metrolink from Manchester Piccadilly: take the Altrincham line to Trafford Bar; ~10 min walk from there.
- The Trafford Centre is nearby — could combine appointment + shopping for the day.
- Nearby budget hotels: Premier Inn Trafford / Holiday Inn Express.
