## What's here

Edinburgh is the smallest UK TLS centre but consistently has the best slot availability. The combination of low demand + central location makes it appealing even for some users south of the border.

## Slot availability patterns

| Season | Typical lead time | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Jan-Feb | Same week ahead | Excellent |
| Mar-Apr | 3-5 days ahead | Excellent |
| May-Aug (peak) | 1-2 weeks ahead | Manageable; better than London/Manchester |
| Sep-Dec | 3-7 days ahead | Excellent |

**The Edinburgh advantage:** even at peak season, Edinburgh usually has slots when London is booked solid 4 weeks out. If you can travel to Edinburgh for the day, the trade-off (train cost vs slot availability) is often worth it.

## Booking calendar

- Opens 14 days ahead.
- Limited operating days (some weeks Mon-Wed only); check carefully.
- Group bookings: limited because the centre is small; large groups (5+) should consider Manchester or split.

## On the day — Edinburgh specifics

- Atholl Crescent is in Edinburgh's West End, residential Georgian terraces.
- The centre is on the ground floor of a converted townhouse; small reception.
- Waiting area: 12-15 seats; intimate. Often quiet.
- Bag check on entry; minimal queue.
- Toilets inside; very small.
- Café within 2 min walk (West End coffee shops on Princes Street).
- No on-site parking. Public transport recommended.

## When to choose Edinburgh

✅ You live in Scotland (default choice)
✅ You're in Newcastle / Northumberland (Edinburgh is closer than Manchester)
✅ You're in London but London is fully booked and you need a slot in 1-2 weeks (train day-trip)
✅ You want the quietest/calmest appointment experience

❌ You're south of the Midlands and London has slots available
❌ You're applying as a family group of 5+ (centre too small)
❌ You need an afternoon slot (Edinburgh closes early)

## Local logistics tips

- LNER train: London King's Cross → Edinburgh Waverley ~4.5h. If booked early, can be £40-70 advance return.
- Haymarket is actually closer to Atholl Crescent than Waverley — get off Haymarket.
- Combine trip: Edinburgh Old Town is 20 min walk from the centre. Plenty to do for a day.
- Nearby budget hotels: Premier Inn Edinburgh Princes Street, Travelodge Princes Street.
- Edinburgh airport: 25 min by tram to city centre.
