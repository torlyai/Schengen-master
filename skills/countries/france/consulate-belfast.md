# TLScontact Belfast (France)

**Address:** Centre Tower, 4-6 Donegall Square North, Belfast BT1 5GR
**Nearest station:** Belfast Lanyon Place (15 min walk) — Great Victoria Street (10 min walk)
**Opening hours:** Tue + Thu 09:00-13:00 (limited days only)
**Collection hours:** Tue + Thu 13:00-15:00
**Coverage:** Northern Ireland (primary); also serves Republic of Ireland applicants via consular agreement
**Last reviewed:** 2026-05-24

## What's here

Belfast is the smallest French TLS centre in the UK and operates only **two days per week** (Tuesday and Thursday). This means slots are scarce despite low total demand — book early.

## Slot availability patterns

| Season | Typical lead time | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Jan-Feb | 1-2 weeks ahead | Limited by 2-day-per-week schedule |
| Mar-Apr | 2-3 weeks ahead | Easter tightens |
| May-Aug (peak) | 3-4 weeks ahead | Tight despite low absolute demand |
| Sep | 1-2 weeks ahead | Slack |
| Oct-Dec | 2 weeks ahead | Steady |

**The Belfast constraint:** only 16-32 appointment slots available per week (vs 100+ in London). Even moderate demand fills the calendar fast.

## Booking calendar

- Opens 14 days ahead (so 4 booking days visible at any time: 2 weeks × 2 days/week).
- Cancellations released ad hoc — Visa Master Premium can catch these.
- Group bookings: very limited; large families should plan well ahead.

## On the day — Belfast specifics

- City centre location; Donegall Square is the central square.
- Centre Tower is a 1970s office block; TLS is on an upper floor (lift in lobby).
- Reception small; waiting area ~10 seats.
- Bag check on entry.
- Toilets inside.
- Cafés abundant on Donegall Place / Royal Avenue (2 min walk).
- No on-site parking; multi-storey car parks nearby (Castlecourt, Victoria Square).

## When to choose Belfast

✅ You live in Northern Ireland (default choice)
✅ You're in the Republic of Ireland and prefer Belfast over the French Embassy Dublin route
✅ You want a quieter, less rushed appointment experience

❌ You're on a tight timeline (only 2 days/week limits options)
❌ You're a group of 4+ (limited capacity per slot)
❌ You need a slot in less than 2 weeks during peak (consider flying to Edinburgh — sometimes cheaper than the lost trip cost from a delayed visa)

## Local logistics tips

- Belfast International / George Best Belfast City airports both well-connected.
- For Republic of Ireland applicants: Dublin → Belfast bus (Aircoach or Translink Goldliner) ~2h.
- The Cathedral Quarter and Titanic Belfast are 20 min walk — combine trip.
- Nearby budget hotels: Premier Inn Belfast City Centre, Ibis Belfast Queens Quarter.
- St George's Market (open Fri-Sun) — worth a visit if your appointment falls near the weekend.
