# TLScontact London (France)

**Address:** 33 Holborn, London EC1N 2HT
**Nearest tube:** Chancery Lane (Central line, 2 min walk) — Holborn (Piccadilly/Central, 5 min)
**Opening hours:** Mon-Fri 08:30-15:30 (last appointments 15:00)
**Collection hours:** Mon-Fri 14:00-16:00
**Coverage:** All UK applicants (especially southern England, London, Wales)
**Last reviewed:** 2026-05-24

## What's here

This is the largest French TLS centre in the UK and handles the most applicants. It's also the most competitive for slot availability, especially May-September.

## Slot availability patterns

| Season | Typical lead time | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Jan-Feb (low season) | 1-2 weeks ahead | Best time to apply |
| Mar-Apr (build-up) | 2-3 weeks ahead | Easter travel demand |
| May-Aug (peak) | 3-4 weeks ahead | Often booked solid; consider `/find-slot` + Visa Master |
| Sep (back-to-school) | 1-2 weeks ahead | Demand drops |
| Oct-Dec (steady) | 2 weeks ahead | Christmas market trips bump December |

## Booking calendar

- Opens 14 days ahead (most slots).
- Some emergency slots released 24-48h ahead — Visa Master Premium can watch for these.
- Group bookings (4+) often need to be sequential individual slots — discuss with TLS support.

## On the day — London specifics

- Tube is faster than bus from most parts of central London.
- The centre is on the 3rd floor of a multi-tenant building; allow 5 min for the lift queue.
- Reception is on the 3rd floor; security check on entry (no airport-grade scanning, just bag inspection).
- Waiting area: 60+ seats; can be standing room only at 09:00 peak.
- Toilets inside the centre; café across the road (Pret a Manger, Starbucks).
- WiFi: TLScontact-Guest network; password from reception.

## When to choose London

✅ You live in London / South East / South West England / Wales
✅ You have flexible dates and the demand period is low
✅ You're applying as part of a group with multiple addresses

❌ You're in Scotland (use Edinburgh)
❌ You're in Northern Ireland (use Belfast)
❌ You need a slot in < 2 weeks during May-Aug (consider Manchester/Edinburgh)

## Local logistics tips

- Park: no on-street; nearest public car park: NCP Drury Lane (£25+ for 2h). Public transport is strongly recommended.
- Stay overnight: nearby budget hotels (Premier Inn Holborn, Travelodge Covent Garden) if your appointment is 09:00 and you're travelling from far.
- Combine trip: TLS appointment morning, sightseeing afternoon (free museums nearby: British Museum, Sir John Soane's).
